# Chocolate-Company-Analysis
This project is a visual and analytical representation of sales and revenue data for a chocolate company. The dashboard, built for business intelligence and reporting, helps track company performance through various KPIs, visualizations, and insights.

# 🍫 Chocolate Company Analysis Dashboard

![Screenshot (1)](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/858c2d72-7892-41f7-8445-b8a570784e1c)

## 📊 Dashboard Overview

The dashboard presents key metrics and visualizations including:

### 🔢 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **Total Revenue:** 4.69M
- **Total Cost:** 1.97M
- **Units Sold:** 1.13M
- **Number of Orders:** 700

### 📦 Revenue by Product
A breakdown of revenue generated by each product:
- Chocolate Chip: 1.7M
- White Chocolate: 1.0M
- Oatmeal Raisin: 0.8M
- Snickerdoodle: 0.6M
- Sugar: 0.5M
- Fortune Cookie: 0.2M

### 🏪 Revenue by Customer Name
Top customers by revenue contribution:
- ACME Bites: 1.4M
- Whole Foods: 1.1M
- ABC Groceries: 0.9M
- Park & Shop Convenience: 0.7M
- Tres Delicious: 0.5M

### 🕓 Revenue by Quarter
- Q1: 0.74M (15.84%)
- Q2: 0.98M (20.87%)
- Q3: 1.01M (21.51%)
- Q4: 1.96M (41.84%)

### 📅 Revenue Over Time
Monthly revenue trends are displayed to show fluctuations and performance across a time span from September 2019 to December 2020.
